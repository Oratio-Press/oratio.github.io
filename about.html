Why Oratio exists

Many Christians want a regular pattern of prayer but do not know how to begin. Work, family, and changing schedules can interrupt a consistent routine of prayer.

Some turn to devotional apps that offer inspiration but little depth. Others discover historic Christian liturgical prayer only to find it difficult to understand and intimidating to begin. Oratio was created to bridge that gap.

Our mission is simple: to help Christians pray Scripture daily through the historic Daily Office, with clear, accessible guidance for every stage of the journey.

What Oratio includes

Guided growth

Start with Scripture & Prayer. Teaching notes and the other Prayer Forms introduce the Psalms, fixed hours, and fuller offices as you use them.

Historic and accessible

The app explains terms and practices from the Daily Office in plain English while preserving the prayers and readings.

Built around habit formation

Short offices and repeatable prayer times give you a pattern you can return to when your schedule changes.

Scripture-centered

The Bible supplies the readings, Psalms, canticles, and prayer language used throughout Oratio.

Our heritage

Oratio is rooted in confessional Lutheran Christianity and the historic Lutheran tradition of daily prayer. Its prayer-office content draws from the Brotherhood Prayer Book, published by Emmanuel Press. This tradition emphasizes:

  • Scripture as the center of prayer
  • daily Psalmody
  • the Church Year as a tool for teaching
  • the patterns of historic Christian worship
  • Christ-centered piety

Oratio gives Christians a place to begin with the Daily Office and a way to learn its full riches.
