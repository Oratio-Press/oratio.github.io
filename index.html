Oratio Prayer App

Pray Scripture daily.

Oratio is a confessional Lutheran prayer app for Christians who want a deeper, more Scripture-centered life of prayer. Start with Scripture and guided prayer, then explore the historic Daily Office through Prayer Forms that meet you where you are.

A deeper prayer life needs a clear place to begin.

Many Christians want a deeper and more consistent life of Scripture and prayer, but they are not sure where to start. Simple devotional routines can feel unstructured. Historic liturgical prayer can feel difficult to enter. Oratio offers another way: begin with Scripture and guided prayer, then discover greater depth as you are ready.
