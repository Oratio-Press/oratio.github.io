A clear path into historic prayer

Most prayer apps give everyone the same experience. Some provide daily inspiration. Others offer full liturgical services and assume users already understand centuries of Christian tradition.

Oratio takes a different approach.

Instead of expecting every user to start at the same place, Oratio is designed to help Christians grow step by step into a richer life of prayer. Whether you're completely new to structured prayer or already familiar with the Daily Office, Oratio can help you build a sustainable discipline of prayer rooted in Scripture.

Five Prayer Forms

At the heart of Oratio is a set of Prayer Forms designed to help Christians grow naturally into the historic practice of the Daily Office. Each Form builds on the one before it. Start with the form that fits your experience and daily schedule.

  1. Scripture & Prayer

    Begin with Scripture and guided prayer. This form establishes a biblical core without requiring previous knowledge of liturgical prayer.

  2. Psalms & Prayer

    Add the Psalms and historic Christian prayers. This form shows how historic prayer grows naturally around Scripture.

  3. Daily Prayer

    Enter the historic rhythm of daily Christian prayer through Morning, Evening, and Night Prayer.

  4. The Daily Offices

    Explore fuller forms of the Church's daily prayer, including richer liturgical structures and additional offices.

  5. The Full Daily Office

    Enter the full richness of the historic Daily Office.

You can explore a fuller form when you're ready, stay where you're comfortable, or return to a simpler form whenever you wish.

Choose a rhythm you can sustain

Many Christians struggle with consistency in prayer. Life becomes busy. Schedules change. Good intentions fade. The Daily Office provides a simple structure that helps overcome these challenges.

While many apps focus on engagement, Oratio focuses on formation. We are not interested in helping people complete a seven-day challenge and then move on. We want to help Christians develop habits that last for decades: a daily rhythm of Scripture, a deeper understanding of prayer, a stronger connection to the historic faith, a life increasingly shaped by God's Word.
