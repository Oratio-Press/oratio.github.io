A clear path into historic prayer

Oratio offers five Prayer Forms. Scripture & Prayer begins with a short reading and guided prayer; fuller forms add Psalms, offices, seasonal material, and teaching.

Choose a Prayer Form, then add the parts of the Daily Office that fit your current schedule and experience. If praying regularly is new to you, begin with Scripture & Prayer. If you already pray the Daily Office, choose a fuller form.

Five Prayer Forms

The five Prayer Forms organize the app’s Scripture readings and Daily Office material. Each Form includes a different amount of Scripture, Psalmody, and prayers. You may choose any Form and change it later. Start with the Form that fits your experience and daily schedule.

  1. Scripture & Prayer

    Begin with a short Scripture reading and guided prayer using Luther’s method of Teaching, Thanksgiving, Confession, and Prayer.

  2. Psalms & Prayer

    Add Psalms and historic Christian prayers.

  3. Daily Prayer

    Pray a simple Morning, Evening, and Night Prayer, with Psalms, readings, hymns, canticles, the Kyrie, the Lord’s Prayer, and the Collect of the Day.

  4. The Daily Offices

    Pray fuller Matins, Vespers, and Compline with readings, antiphons, hymns, responsories, canticles, collects, and versicles.

  5. The Full Daily Office

    Pray the complete Daily Office, with prayers for every time of day.

Use one Form consistently, try another, or return to a shorter Form when your schedule changes.

Choose a rhythm you can sustain

Schedules change. Oratio gives you a defined place to begin, with short and fuller Prayer Forms for different amounts of time. You can return after an interruption at any time without any need to catch up.
