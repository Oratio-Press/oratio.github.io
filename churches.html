A practical companion to the local church

Most Christians spend only a small portion of their week at church. The rest of their spiritual formation happens at home, at work, during commutes, around family meals, and in the ordinary routines of daily life.

Oratio helps Christians carry a pattern of Scripture and prayer into the days between services.

Pastors and church leaders can recommend Oratio as a personal resource for Scripture, Psalmody, and daily prayer.

A tool for prayer during the week

Oratio gives church members Scripture readings and prayer offices to use during the week. The resource may be useful for:

  • Christians beginning a daily prayer routine
  • families praying at home
  • congregations introducing historic Christian prayer

Members can use Oratio between services for Scripture readings, Psalms, and prayer. It does not replace preaching, pastoral care, worship, or the sacraments.

Share Oratio with your congregation

Oratio is a personal prayer resource for use alongside congregational worship, preaching, pastoral care, catechesis, and the sacraments. If you are a pastor or church leader and want to discuss how to introduce Oratio, contact us.
