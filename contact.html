Our mission

Oratio exists to help Christians discover a deeper, Scripture-shaped life of prayer by making the historic Daily Office understandable, accessible, and practicable.

To help Christians pray the Scriptures daily and recover the treasures of historic Christian prayer, devotion, and spiritual formation.

Everything we create, from educational resources to the Oratio Prayer App itself, is intended to serve that goal. We believe that lives shaped by God's Word are transformed over time through faithful habits of prayer, worship, and meditation on Scripture. And we'd be honored to help you on that journey.
