Answers about Oratio and the Daily Office

These questions address how to begin, what historic prayer means, how Oratio is rooted in confessional Lutheran Christianity, and how the app supports a sustainable rhythm of Scripture and prayer. If you do not find what you are looking for, contact us or join the launch list for future updates.

About Oratio

What is Oratio?

Oratio is a confessional Lutheran prayer app that helps Christians build a deeper, Scripture-centered life of prayer through the historic Daily Office. It begins with Scripture and guided prayer, then opens greater depth through Prayer Forms.

What makes Oratio different?

Oratio does not ask you to choose between accessible biblical prayer and the richness of historic Christian tradition. It begins simply and introduces the Daily Office as you are ready.

Is Oratio a devotional app?

Oratio helps Christians make Scripture and prayer part of daily life. Its purpose is not merely to provide inspirational content, but to support a deeper, sustainable rhythm of Christian prayer.

Getting started

I do not know how to pray. Where should I begin?

Begin with Scripture & Prayer. You do not need to know liturgical terminology, the Church Year, or how to chant before you begin. Oratio introduces unfamiliar practices clearly and gradually.

What if I already pray the Daily Office?

Oratio can support Christians who are already familiar with the Daily Office. Choose the Prayer Form that fits your experience and explore a fuller form when you are ready.

I do not have much time. Can I still use Oratio?

Yes. Choose a Prayer Form, office, and schedule appropriate to your life. Oratio encourages a sustainable rhythm rather than overwhelming you with a long commitment.

Theology & tradition

Is Oratio Lutheran?

Oratio is rooted in confessional Lutheran Christianity and the historic Lutheran tradition of daily prayer. Its prayer-office content draws from the Brotherhood Prayer Book, published by Emmanuel Press.

Can Christians from other traditions use Oratio?

Yes. Oratio is distinctly rooted in confessional Lutheran Christianity and genuinely welcomes Christians from other backgrounds who want to discover this way of prayer.

What is the Daily Office?

The Daily Office is a historic pattern of prayer built around Scripture, Psalms, readings, canticles, and prayers offered at regular times throughout the day. Christians have practiced various forms of the Daily Office for centuries as a way of ordering daily life around God's Word.

Features

What prayer offices will be included?

Oratio will include all seven offices of the historic Daily Office. Additional resources and prayer content may be added over time.

Does Oratio include Scripture readings?

Yes. Scripture is at the center of the Oratio experience. Daily readings, Psalms, biblical canticles, and Scripture-based prayers are woven throughout the app.

Will there be audio at launch?

Audio availability is still subject to the final launch plan. If audio is included, it will support guided spoken or sung prayer rather than replace the practice itself.

Does Oratio follow the Church Year?

Yes. Oratio incorporates the seasons and rhythms of the historic Christian calendar, helping users engage more deeply with the life of Christ throughout the year, through seasons such as:

  • Advent
  • Christmas
  • Epiphany
  • Lent
  • Easter
  • Pentecost
  • Trinity season
Will there be educational content?

Yes. Oratio will include articles, guides, and explanations designed to help users understand:

  • Prayer
  • The Psalms
  • The Daily Office
  • Liturgical worship
  • The Church Year
  • Historic Christian practices

Learning is integrated throughout the experience.

Subscription & pricing

What is free, and what supports continued development?

The free experience is intended to provide a meaningful way to build a daily practice of Scripture and prayer. Premium access, if offered, supports the continued development and maintenance of the app.

What will premium access include?

Premium access is expected to provide greater depth and additional functionality. Final pricing and the exact free/premium boundary will be stated clearly before launch.

Will there be a lifetime purchase option?

We are exploring a lifetime access option for users who prefer a one-time purchase rather than an ongoing subscription. More details will be announced closer to launch.

Accounts & technology

What devices will be supported?

Oratio is being developed for iPhone and Android.

Will my progress sync across devices?

Yes, if you choose. An account is optional: you can pray without one. With an account, you can turn on sync to keep your prayer records on your other devices. See our privacy policy for what is kept.

Will I need an internet connection to pray?

No. Every office works offline: the texts are in the app. A connection is needed only for optional features, such as keeping an account in sync.

Families & churches

Can families use Oratio together?

Yes. Many of the practices encouraged within Oratio naturally support family prayer and devotional life. We also plan to provide resources specifically aimed at helping households establish healthy spiritual rhythms. Other community features are planned for the future.

Can churches recommend Oratio to their members?

Absolutely. Oratio was designed to complement the ministry of the local church by encouraging consistent engagement with Scripture and prayer throughout the week. Churches may find it useful for:

  • Family ministry
  • Discipleship efforts
  • Small groups
  • Catechesis
  • Seasonal devotions
