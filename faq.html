About Oratio

What is Oratio?

Oratio is a prayer app that helps Christians build a deeper, Scripture-centered life of prayer through the historic Daily Office. It begins with Scripture and guided prayer. Other Prayer Forms add Psalms, fixed prayer times, teaching notes, and fuller offices.

What makes Oratio different?

Oratio begins with biblical prayer and gives you a way to learn the historic Daily Office. Choose Scripture & Prayer for a short beginning, or choose a fuller Prayer Form if you already know the Daily Office.

Is Oratio a devotional app?

Oratio helps Christians make Scripture and prayer part of daily life. Oratio provides readings and prayers to support a deeper, sustainable routine of Christian prayer.

When will Oratio launch?

Oratio is in development and planned for a Lent 2027 launch window. The exact store date depends on final product, content, review, and support checks.

Getting started

I do not know how to pray. Where should I begin?

Begin with Scripture & Prayer, and move on to fuller Prayer Forms as you get familiar. Teaching notes explain terms such as collect, versicle, and responsory within the relevant Prayer Form.

What if I already pray the Daily Office?

Oratio can support Christians who are already familiar with the Daily Office. Choose the Prayer Form that best fits your previous experience, such as The Daily Offices or The Full Daily Office.

I do not have much time. Can I still use Oratio?

Yes. Choose a Prayer Form, office, and schedule appropriate to your life. Choose a shorter or fuller office according to the time you have that day.

Theology & tradition

What is the Daily Office?

The Daily Office is a historic pattern of prayer built around Scripture, Psalms, and prayers offered at regular times throughout the day. Christians have practiced various forms of the Daily Office for centuries as a way of ordering daily life around God's Word.

Is Oratio Lutheran?

Oratio is rooted in confessional Lutheran Christianity and the historic Lutheran tradition of daily prayer. Its prayer-office content draws from the Brotherhood Prayer Book, published by Emmanuel Press.

Can Christians from other traditions use Oratio?

Yes. Oratio is intended for any Christian who wants to pray Scripture daily and learn the Daily Office.

Features

What prayer offices will be included?

The five Prayer Forms provide increasing amounts of Daily Office material. The Full Daily Office includes all seven offices of the historic Daily Office.

Does Oratio include Scripture readings?

Yes. The app includes daily Bible readings, Psalms, biblical canticles, and Scripture-based prayers.

Will there be audio?

Yes. Audio features for sung or chanted elements are included with The Daily Offices and The Full Daily Office, which are available for a one-time $9.99 purchase.

Does Oratio follow the Church Year?

Yes. Church Year material supplies seasonal readings, colors, collects, hymns, and teaching notes according to the Prayer Form.

Will there be educational content?

Yes. Oratio will include short teaching notes designed to help users understand unfamiliar prayer terms, and practices as they appear. An article library will also provide longer guides and explanations for other topics related to prayer, the Daily Office, and historic Christian worship.

Pricing

Which Prayer Forms are free?

Scripture & Prayer, Psalms & Prayer, and Daily Prayer are free. The Daily Offices and The Full Daily Office require a one-time $9.99 purchase, which supports the continued development and maintenance of the app.

How much do the paid Prayer Forms cost?

Access to the paid Prayer Forms will be set at a one-time purchase of $9.99.

What does the one-time purchase include?

The one-time purchase unlocks The Daily Offices and The Full Daily Office. Depending on the Form, those including audio features, additional prayer offices, memory work incorporated into the offices, Book of Concord readings, and additional Scripture readings.

Accounts & technology

What devices will be supported?

Oratio is being developed for iPhone and Android.

Will my progress sync across devices?

Yes, if you choose. An account is optional: you can pray without one. With an account, you can turn on sync to keep your prayer records on your other devices. See our privacy policy for what is kept.

Will I need an internet connection to pray?

No. Every office works offline: the texts are in the app. A connection is needed only for downloading recordings ahead of time and for optional features, such as keeping an account in sync.

Families & churches

Can families use Oratio together?

Yes. Families can use the Scripture readings, Psalms, and prayer offices for household prayer. Community features are planned for the future.

Can churches recommend Oratio to their members?

Yes. Oratio is a personal prayer resource to use alongside congregational worship, preaching, pastoral care, catechesis, and the sacraments. See Oratio for churches for more information.

What resources will be available for churches?

Oratio Press anticipates expanding into additional educational and ministry resources over time, though specific future offerings have not yet been announced. See Oratio for churches for more information.
