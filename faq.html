Here are short answers to questions about Oratio and the Daily Office. If you do not find what you are looking for, contact us or join the launch list for future updates.

About Oratio

What is Oratio?

Oratio is a prayer app that helps Christians build a deeper, Scripture-centered life of prayer through the historic Daily Office. It begins with Scripture and guided prayer, then builds depth through Prayer Forms that enrich the basics.

What makes Oratio different?

Oratio does not ask you to choose between accessible biblical prayer and the richness of historic Christian tradition. It begins where you are and introduces the Daily Office as you are ready to build up a deeper prayer routine.

Is Oratio a devotional app?

Oratio helps Christians make Scripture and prayer part of daily life. Its purpose is not merely to provide inspirational content, but to support a deeper, sustainable routine of Christian prayer.

When will Oratio launch?

Oratio is currently under development, with launch tentatively planned for early 2027. Join the launch list for announcements about launch milestones and availability.

Getting started

I do not know how to pray. Where should I begin?

Begin with Scripture & Prayer. You do not need to know liturgical terminology, the Church Year, or how to chant before you begin. Oratio introduces unfamiliar practices clearly and gradually.

What if I already pray the Daily Office?

Oratio can support Christians who are already familiar with the Daily Office. Choose the Prayer Form that fits your experience and explore a fuller form when you are ready.

I do not have much time. Can I still use Oratio?

Yes. Choose a Prayer Form, office, and schedule appropriate to your life. Oratio encourages a sustainable rhythm rather than overwhelming you with a long commitment.

Theology & tradition

What is the Daily Office?

The Daily Office is a historic pattern of prayer built around Scripture, Psalms, and prayers offered at regular times throughout the day. Christians have practiced various forms of the Daily Office for centuries as a way of ordering daily life around God's Word.

Is Oratio Lutheran?

Oratio is rooted in confessional Lutheran Christianity and the historic Lutheran tradition of daily prayer. Its prayer-office content draws from the Brotherhood Prayer Book, published by Emmanuel Press.

Can Christians from other traditions use Oratio?

Yes. Oratio is designed for any Christian who wants to develop a deeper, Scripture-centered life of prayer and who wants to learn more about historic Christian prayer.

Features

What prayer offices will be included?

Oratio will include all seven offices of the historic Daily Office. Additional resources and prayer content may be added over time.

Does Oratio include Scripture readings?

Yes. Scripture is at the center of Oratio's approach to prayer. Daily readings, Psalms, biblical canticles, and Scripture-based prayers are woven throughout the app.

Will there be audio?

Yes, audio of sung or chanted elements of the Daily Office will be included in the premium version of the app.

Does Oratio follow the Church Year?

Yes. Oratio incorporates the seasons and rhythms of the historic Christian calendar, helping users to follow the life of Christ throughout the year. The amount of Church Year detail will vary by Prayer Form.

Will there be educational content?

Yes. Oratio will include short teaching notes designed to help users understand unfamiliar prayer terms, and practices as they appear. An article library will also provide longer guides and explanations for other topics related to prayer, the Daily Office, and historic Christian worship.

Pricing

What is free, and what supports continued development?

The first three Prayer Forms are provided completely free, and are intended to provide a meaningful way to build a daily practice of Scripture reading and prayer. Premium access supports the continued development and maintenance of the app.

How much will premium cost?

Premium access will be set at a one-time purchase of $9.99.

What will premium access include?

The purchase provides The Daily Offices and The Full Daily Office from the five Prayer Forms, including audio features, more prayer offices, memory work incorporated into the offices, Book of Concord readings at Vigils, and more Scripture readings.

Accounts & technology

What devices will be supported?

Oratio is being developed for iPhone and Android.

Will my progress sync across devices?

Yes, if you choose. An account is optional: you can pray without one. With an account, you can turn on sync to keep your prayer records on your other devices. See our privacy policy for what is kept.

Will I need an internet connection to pray?

No. Every office works offline: the texts are in the app. A connection is needed only for dowloading recordings ahead of time and for optional features, such as keeping an account in sync.

Families & churches

Can families use Oratio together?

Yes. Many of the practices encouraged within Oratio naturally support family prayer and devotional life. We also plan to provide resources specifically aimed at helping households establish healthy spiritual disciplines. Other community features are planned for the future.

Can churches recommend Oratio to their members?

Absolutely. Oratio was designed to complement,not replace the ministry of the local church by encouraging consistent engagement with Scripture and prayer throughout the week. See Oratio for churches for more information.

What resources will be available for churches?

Oratio Press anticipates expanding into additional educational and ministry resources over time, though specific future offerings have not yet been announced. See Oratio for churches for more information.
